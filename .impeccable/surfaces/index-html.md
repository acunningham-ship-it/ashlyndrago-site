# Surface: ashlyndrago.com (whole site, index.html first)

Scope: the portfolio site. Home is the first surface, plus the art, acting, about and contact sections. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads from the first viewport).
Audience and job: people Ashlyn sends her link to, usually on a phone. In seconds they should see who she is, then browse her art.
Constraints: user-pinned dark ground and dark blue accent; her provided photo is the home header; no invented credits.
Memorable moment: her photo is frame 01 on a film strip, and scrolling advances the strip frame by frame through her work.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Ashlyn's portfolio is a hand-processed 16mm strip. Her photo is the first exposed frame and every artwork is a numbered frame after it. It refuses the category default of a full-bleed photo hero over a masonry grid.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black leader (#0a0b0e) and shadow gray (#1a1c21) as ground, silver-gray emulsion text (#c9ced6 / #8a919c). The light leak is translated from orange to **pinned deep blue**: a #1e3c8c→#4f74e0 bloom reserved for the active frame and primary actions. Stamped condensed caps for edge codes (Big Shoulders), grease-pencil numerals (handwritten face) for frame numbers and her notes, plus a quiet grotesk for reading. Sprocket holes, edge-code margins and frame rules make up all the chrome, and buttons are strip-ends with sprockets.

STORY: The visitor meets Ashlyn (frame 01). They read "actress & digital artist", scroll the strip through her art, reach the "next reel" acting frame (stage work not yet shot, presented as an unexposed frame waiting), and leave through the stage door: ashlynjd@gmail.com.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: stamped edge-code rail ("ASHLYN DRAGO ▸ 01 ▸ ACTRESS ▸ DIGITAL ARTIST"). Left-center: a vertical film strip with her photo as frame 01, blue bloom on its edge, frame 02 (her art) peeking below the fold. Right: FRAME 01 kicker, ASHLYN DRAGO in huge stamped caps, a one-line intro, and nav as underlined caps links (Art, Gallery, Acting, About). Primary action: "View the reel" strip button. On phones, the strip runs full width with the name over the top.

FORM: Hand-processed film bloom (challenger, catalog cinema-cinematography-editing-hand-processed-film-bloom), chosen by the user over assigned candidate 3 (black-paper sketchbook). seed:b1065635db543e2d. Signature interaction: the active frame in the viewport takes the blue light-leak bloom while a frame counter (FRAME 04 OF 21) updates; the lightbox is a projector gate with prev and next sprockets. Motion: frames develop from fogged to clear as they enter; it's disabled under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

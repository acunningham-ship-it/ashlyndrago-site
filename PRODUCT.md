# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. GitHub repo (account `acunningham-ship-it`), hosted on GitHub Pages, custom domain `ashlyndrago.com` (DNS in Cloudflare, Hamstudios101 account).

## Users

- Primary: people Ashlyn shares her link with: casting contacts, drama teachers and directors, school and college admissions or portfolio reviewers, people who might commission art, and friends and family. They come to see who she is and what she has made.
- Ashlyn herself: the site is a surprise gift from Armani. It has to feel like *hers*, a place she's proud to send people.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Ashlyn Jolene Drago, actress and digital artist. It brings her work out of a Wix template site (ashlyndrago1.wixsite.com/my-site-2) and a Canva portfolio (ethersonmastery2025.my.canva.site/dada3-ashlyn-jolene-drago-portfolio) into one site she owns at ashlyndrago.com. Success: she's delighted when she sees it, and anyone she sends it to understands within seconds who she is and can browse her art.

## Positioning

Two sides, one person: an actress at the start of her stage career and a working digital artist. The art is the proof on hand today. The acting is presented honestly as where she's headed.

## Operating Context

Shared mostly as a link on phones (texts, social bios, applications). Sometimes viewed on a laptop by a reviewer or teacher.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static pages: home (her photo as the header image), art gallery, acting, about, contact.
- Contact: `ashlynjd@gmail.com` (mailto). No form.
- Must stay maintainable by hand: images in `assets/`, content in plain HTML.

## Brand Commitments

- User-pinned: **dark background with a dark blue accent.**
- User-pinned: the provided photo of Ashlyn (`assets/img/ashlyn-original.jpg`) goes on the home page header.
- Name: Ashlyn Jolene Drago, "Ashlyn Drago."

## Evidence on Hand

- Her photo: `assets/img/ashlyn-original.jpg` (casual full-length shot in a school hallway, black dress with a red cherry print).
- Her artwork and text, scraped from the Wix and Canva sites into `_source/` (see `_source/content.md`).
- **No stage or screen credits exist yet. Never invent plays, roles, training, awards, or testimonials.**

## Product Principles

1. The work leads. Her art is the main content; the interface stays out of its way.
2. Honest about where she is: "emerging" is a strength, not something to hide.
3. Personal, not a template. It should read as Ashlyn, not as a Wix theme.
4. Fast and readable on a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on the dark ground; alt text on all artwork; respect reduced motion.
